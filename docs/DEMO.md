# Demo and hackathon checklist

## A 2–3 minute product demonstration

1. **Problem (15s):** low usage is not necessarily low value. Freelancers need to
   remember why a tool mattered before another renewal, not just see a list.
2. **Access + overview (30s):** show wallet connection, signed login and the
   encrypted Mainnet vault without revealing keys/passwords. Show calculated USD commitments, budget headroom, and next
   renewals. Clearly identify sample data or use your own synthetic workspace.
3. **Learning (50s):** add a rarely used, optional client design tool. Its initial
   recommendation is downgrade. Review it: keep, essential value, “required for
   monthly client handoffs.” Return to Overview: compare downgrade without memory
   to keep with context. Show the journal and reload to demonstrate persistence.
4. **Fresh facts (20s):** edit the price upward by more than 10%. Show fresh-review
   advice rather than blindly repeating old feedback. Essential protection and
   budget do not change automatically.
5. **Real memory (30s, after live verification):** explicitly upload a synthetic
   decision, show its pending job, then a completed blob receipt. Recall it and
   show it informing the recommendation. An accepted job is not Mainnet proof.
6. **Tatum (20s, after live verification):** verify a real native SUI payment's
   recipient, amount, date, execution status, and optional sender. Explain that a
   generic transaction lookup or USD service entry is not payment verification.
7. **Close (10s):** local-first decisions, explicit external memory, no autonomous
   financial action. Do not present pending integrations as working evidence.

Optional AI segment: use synthetic context only. If strict privacy has no free
endpoint, explicitly check the assistant's demo-data opt-in (providers may retain
or train on that data), then confirm sharing for that request. The option resets
afterward. Free-only caps remain enforced; OpenRouter account Privacy settings can
still block it. Do not suggest relaxed privacy is required by the hackathon rules.

## Owner tasks before submission

- [ ] Confirm SpendGuard reuse permission; add the author's preferred attribution
      and applicable licence before publishing.
- [ ] Sign in with your own Mainnet Sui wallet and configure your Mainnet Tatum
      key, wallet-owned MemWal account and registered delegate in the encrypted vault.
      Never use the friend's credentials or an owner wallet private key.
- [ ] Explicitly authorise real synthetic test uploads and any associated cost.
- [ ] Test a supported authenticated Tatum Sui query/payment end to end. Adjust
      the query if the gateway's live schema differs from installed declarations.
- [ ] Store at least **10 meaningful, genuine, unique Walrus Mainnet memories**.
      Use different legitimate decision contexts, not duplicated qualification spam.
      Local/sample examples and accepted/pending jobs never count.
- [ ] Retain completed blob/job evidence, export the profile backup, restart,
      recall relevant memories, and demonstrate changed recommendations. Retrieval
      count is bounded, not an account-wide total.
- [ ] Resolve dependency/security findings and perform visible browser review.
- [ ] Deploy built assets with `npm start`, configure the exact HTTPS public origin, and use only
      synthetic data in the public demo. Do not publicly unlock private owner data.
- [ ] Publish the public repository and working demo.
- [ ] Publish the blog post and 2–3 minute video with real integration evidence.
- [ ] Confirm current organiser rules and AI Builder bonus eligibility.
- [ ] Submit before **10 November 2026, 17:00 UTC**, subject to organiser updates.
